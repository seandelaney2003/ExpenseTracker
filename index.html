How this calendar works: any day shows each income/expense item with that Due day set in Budget, listed separately — nothing is added together on a plain day. Hover or tap a day to see the full list plus its total in a tooltip. Fridays are different: they show your whole week's income minus expenses added up, marked with Σ so a real sum is never confused with the individual amounts on other days. A green dot on a Friday means something is also specifically due that day. An amber dot means that week has an unpaid expense. Tap any day to jump to its week in the spreadsheet below.

Variance vs. anticipated
Starting balance is locked in the first time you open this month — your latest balance snapshot from before the month began, or your current accounts total if there wasn't one yet. Estimated ending balance = starting balance + planned income − planned expenses for every week in the month. Actual balance (latest) is the most recent balance you've entered in this month (Weekly, Monthly, or Budget), or your live current total if you haven't entered one yet. Variance = actual − estimated: green means you're ahead of plan, red means behind.

Expense Categories
Recurring categories: set the amount once here and it applies automatically — pick Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly (which Friday of the month) below the toggle, and off-schedule weeks plan for $0 unless you type something in that one week. Edit a week's own box in Weekly or Monthly if you know just that week will differ. Turn "Recurring" off for things that change too often to plan (balancing accounts, etc.) — those have no default, so the box here sets just the current week's amount. For expenses, the small Due day box (1–31) is just a reference reminder — it shows up next to the name in Weekly and Monthly.
