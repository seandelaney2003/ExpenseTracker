How it works

Three steps. Set it up once, then spend a minute a week keeping it current.

1

Set up your categories, once

Add your paychecks and bills, set how often each repeats — weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, bi‑annually, or yearly — and the app builds your whole forecast automatically.

2

Check things off as they happen

Got paid? Paid a bill? Tap the checkbox. The amount locks in as what actually happened, right on the week it landed — your history never silently shifts later.

3

See your real forecast

Your dashboard shows what you have today, what's left to collect and pay this month, and where your balance is projected to land — instantly, with no math.
